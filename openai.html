A quick FAQ about AI—and about this independent website concept.

Is this the official OpenAI website?＋

No. This is an independent fan-made design concept and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. For official information, visit openai.com.

Can AI make mistakes?＋

Yes. AI systems can produce inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information. Verify important details with trustworthy sources, especially for consequential decisions.

How can I get better results from AI?＋

State your goal, share relevant context, specify the format you need, and ask the system to explain assumptions or revise its answer.

Does this concept provide AI services?＋

No. It is a static visual website and does not connect to OpenAI models, accounts, or APIs.
